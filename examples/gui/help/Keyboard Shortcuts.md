# Keyboard Shortcuts

[[toc]]

## Moving

| Keys | What they do |
|------|--------------|
| Up, Down | scroll a little |
| Page Up, Page Down, Space | scroll a page |
| Home, End | the top, the bottom |

## Pages

| Keys | What they do |
|------|--------------|
| Alt+Left (Cmd+[) | back |
| Alt+Right (Cmd+]) | forward |

## Text

- **Ctrl+C** (Cmd+C) copies the selection.
- **Ctrl+A** (Cmd+A) selects everything.
